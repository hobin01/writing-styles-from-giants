## Request

Write only the opening paragraph of a decision note. Should the lab replace both readers now? Reader A failed on 3 of 20 trial runs. Reader B passed all 20. The team has funding to replace one reader. Replacement reliability has not been tested. The lab may repeat the trial next week if the bench is free.

## Checks set before the runs

Answer the decision within one opening paragraph. Relate the budget to both trial records. Distinguish the measured failures from replacement reliability, and keep next week’s trial conditional.

## Without this skill

Keep Reader B and defer the decision on Reader A. Reader A failed on 3 of 20 trial runs, while Reader B passed all 20. ## With this skill

No. Keep Reader B and investigate A’s failures before spending the available funds. If the bench is free next week, the lab may repeat the trial.
