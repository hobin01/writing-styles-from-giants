Why one extra minute can cost more than a minute

Earth sends at minute 0. The rover waits 10 minutes after receiving the signal to prepare, then can begin its reply at the first 25-minute slot at or after readiness. The return trip takes the same time as the outward trip.

Two one-way-delay cases (all times in minutes after Earth sends)
One-way delay Rover receives Ready after 10 min Reply starts at eligible slot Earth receives reply
15 min 0 + 15 = 15 15 + 10 = 25 25 25 + 15 = 40
16 min 0 + 16 = 16 16 + 10 = 26 50 50 + 16 = 66

Difference in Earth’s reply receipt time: 66 − 40 = 26 minutes.

At 15 minutes, the rover is ready exactly at a reply slot, so it can send then. The threshold is met on time.

The one-minute increase moves readiness past the 25-minute slot. Since the next permitted slot is much later, the reply waits for that slot; the threshold crossing creates the extra delay.
