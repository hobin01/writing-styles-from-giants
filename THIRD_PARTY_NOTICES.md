# Third-party sources and rights

This foundation contains original project documents, small original examples, and links to external material. It does not bundle external books, ASD-STE100 dictionaries, institutional checklist texts, or existing skill implementations.

The rules in the authoring example are explicitly project-original. No thinker, historical figure, book, company, or institution is presented as their source. Future methods may draw on any of these source categories, subject to the same evidence and rights review.

Future methods must record their provenance and rights in method metadata. Future reused code must list the upstream project, version/commit, files, license, required notice, and modifications here. Retain file-level licensing exceptions; do not assume an entire repository or its references share one license.
